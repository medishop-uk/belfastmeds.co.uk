Is it safe to order medicines online from BelfastMeds?

Yes. Our ordering system is secure, and every prescription is checked by a qualified UK-registered pharmacist before any order is approved and dispatched.

Do I need to visit a pharmacy in person?

No. You can upload your NHS or private prescription online and complete the full ordering process from home.

Will my parcel be discreet?

Yes. All orders are sent in plain, unbranded packaging, so no one can tell what is inside.

Are your medicines genuine and UK-approved?

Yes. We only source from authorised UK wholesalers, and all medicines meet strict MHRA standards for safety and authenticity.

Can I contact someone if I need help?

Yes. Our support team and UK-registered pharmacists are available to answer your questions. You can get in touch by phone or email for assistance.

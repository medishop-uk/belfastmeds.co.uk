The hidden danger of counterfeit medicines

One of the serious issues related to chasing medicines from online platforms is the existence of counterfeit medicines or fake drugs that do not follow the inclusion of necessary ingredients and sometimes contain harmful ingredients. These are fake products that look like actual medicines. There are many risks associated to counterfeit medicines or fake drugs like –

·       Interruption of the actual treatment

·       Collapse the actual treatment

·       Worsening medical condition of the patient

·       Causing unexpected side effects

·       Causing allergic reactions.

People who consume counterfeit medicines or fake drugs may suffer from one or many of these health-related issues while delaying their actual treatment.

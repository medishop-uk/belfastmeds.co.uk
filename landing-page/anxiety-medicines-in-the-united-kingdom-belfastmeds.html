Anxiety & panic disorder

Anxiety and panic disorder medications may help reduce excessive worry, panic attacks, and physical symptoms. The generic medications are diazepam, alprazolam, escitalopram, and sertraline.

Anxiety & seizure disorder

Certain drugs are used to stop seizures, and they may also help treat certain anxiety-related conditions when taken under a doctor's supervision. Common generic drugs include clonazepam, gabapentin, pregabalin, and lamotrigine.

Nerve-pain & anxiety-related medicines 

There are some medications that can help with the treatment of anxiety and also help with nerve pain in the right patients. Common generic drugs include pregabalin, gabapentin, duloxetine, and amitriptyline.

  • Buy only from a trusted online pharmacy.
  • Check that the medicine packaging is sealed.
  • Look for clear expiration and batch numbers.
  • Avoid websites offering unrealistic discounts.
  • Read product information carefully.
  • Contact the pharmacy if you have questions before ordering.

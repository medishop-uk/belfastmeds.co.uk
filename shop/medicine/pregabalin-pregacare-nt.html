Pregabalin (Pregacare NT)

Pregacare-NT helps relieve neuropathic pain from diabetes-related nerve damage and shingles-related nerve pain. It uses Mecobalamin, Pregabalin, and Nortriptyline to repair damaged nerves and ease pain. Some people may experience dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, blurred vision, or swelling, which often improves as the body adjusts.

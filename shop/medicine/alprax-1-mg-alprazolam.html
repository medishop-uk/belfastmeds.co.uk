Anti-anxiety drug alprazolam (Alprax) prices in the UK

Alprax 1 mg is a fast-acting medication used to manage anxiety, panic attacks, and stress-related sleep problems. It helps reduce nervous tension and promotes a sense of calm by slowing overactive brain signals.

  • Drug Class: Benzodiazepine
  • Active Ingredient: Alprazolam
  • Available Strengths: 1 mg, 2 mg
  • Forms: Tablet, bar, powder
  • Uses: Anxiety, panic disorder, seizures
  • Common Side Effects: Drowsiness, dizziness, fatigue, poor coordination, memory issues, and confusion.

Description

Alprax is a prescription medicine used for the short-term management of anxiety and panic disorders. It helps reduce excessive nervous system activity that causes fear, restlessness, and tension. This medicine is intended for short-term use under close medical supervision.

How Alprax Works

Alprazolam increases the effect of GABA, a calming neurotransmitter in the brain. Its sedative effects depend on the dose and can range from mild relaxation to deep sedation. By slowing brain activity, it helps control anxiety and panic symptoms effectively.

Dosage

Start with 0.25–0.5 mg up to three times daily. Increase gradually if required, without exceeding 4 mg per day. Always taper slowly when discontinuing. Always taper slowly when discontinuing to avoid withdrawal symptoms.

Warnings

Not recommended for children. Avoid use during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Long-term or improper use may lead to dependence or tolerance.

Overdose Risk

Excessive use can lead to severe drowsiness, confusion, and coma. Emergency care is required. Risk increases significantly when combined with alcohol or other sedatives.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place below 30°C. Keep away from children. Protect from moisture, heat, and direct sunlight.

UK Regulation

Alprax (Alprazolam) is classified as a Prescription Only Medicine (POM) in the United Kingdom. It can only be supplied with a valid prescription issued by a UK-registered doctor or qualified prescriber.

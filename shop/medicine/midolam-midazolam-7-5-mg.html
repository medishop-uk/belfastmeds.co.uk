Description

Midolam (Midazolam) 7.5 mg is a sedative medication used for anxiety, sleep, and procedures. It helps patients relax before medical interventions. Its rapid onset makes it suitable for short-term use.

How It Works

The drug enhances GABA-mediated inhibition in the brain. This decreases excessive neural activity. Sedation and relaxation follow quickly after administration.

Dosage

Dosage varies based on patient condition and purpose of use. For insomnia, it is taken shortly before bedtime. Medical procedures require professional administration.

Side Effects

Drowsiness and fatigue are commonly experienced. Memory impairment may occur temporarily. Severe effects are rare but possible with improper use.

Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

Midazolam is not routinely recommended during pregnancy. It may cause harm if used repeatedly. Breastfeeding should be avoided during treatment.

Storage

Store at room temperature in a dry environment. Avoid storing near heat or sunlight. Keep medication securely stored.

UK Regulation

Midazolam is a controlled prescription medication in the UK. It is listed under Schedule 3 regulations. Dispensing requires a valid medical prescription.

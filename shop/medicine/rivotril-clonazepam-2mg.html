Description

Rivotril is a clonazepam-based benzodiazepine used in panic and seizure management. It helps control anxiety episodes and abnormal neurological activity. The 2 mg formulation is typically used for established treatment regimens.

How It Works

Clonazepam enhances the calming effects of GABA in the central nervous system. This suppresses excessive neuronal activity in the brain. It leads to reduced seizure frequency and panic symptoms.

Dosage

Dosage is individualized according to diagnosis and patient tolerance. Physicians usually increase doses gradually to reduce side effects. Rivotril 2 mg should be taken exactly as directed.

Side Effects

Common reactions include tiredness, dizziness, and coordination difficulties. Chronic use may result in dependence and withdrawal effects. Severe side effects may include respiratory depression and confusion.

Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

Use during pregnancy may carry risks to fetal development. Clonazepam enters breast milk and may affect nursing babies. Medical advice is required before use in these situations.

Storage

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep the medicine in its original packaging. Store securely and out of children’s reach.

UK Regulation

Clonazepam is classified as a Schedule 4 controlled drug in the UK. It is supplied only as a Prescription Only Medicine. Regulation exists to limit misuse and dependence.

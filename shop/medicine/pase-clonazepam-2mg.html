Description

Pase (Clonazepam) 2 mg is used for panic-related anxiety and seizure control. It is particularly useful in severe or resistant epilepsy cases. Long-term benefits must be reviewed periodically.

How It Works

The drug strengthens the brain’s natural calming chemical, GABA. This reduces panic intensity and seizure activity. It works by slowing excessive nerve impulses.

Dosage

Doctors start low and increase cautiously. Daily dose depends on medical condition and response. Never stop suddenly without medical advice.

Side Effects

Common effects include tiredness and reduced alertness. Mood changes and confusion may occur. Risk of addiction exists with prolonged use.

Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

Potential risks to unborn babies have been reported. Drug passes into breast milk. Use only if no safer alternative exists.

Storage

Store at controlled room temperature. Protect from light and moisture. Secure storage is essential.

UK Regulation

Legal only with a valid prescription. Controlled under Schedule 4 regulations. Non-medical use is unlawful.

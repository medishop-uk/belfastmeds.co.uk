Description

Alprax 2 mg helps control panic attacks and ongoing anxiety by calming an overstimulated brain, allowing patients to feel more at ease and in control. It is commonly prescribed for short-term management of panic and anxiety disorders. Long-term use should be carefully reviewed due to the risk of dependence.

How It Works

It boosts GABA activity, which slows excessive brain signals and encourages relaxation. This reduces feelings of fear, restlessness, and physical tension. The calming effect varies depending on dose and individual response.

Recommended Dosage

Begin with a low dose taken two to three times daily. Dose changes should be gradual, and sudden stopping should be avoided. Dose changes should be gradual, and sudden stopping should be avoided. Abrupt discontinuation may cause withdrawal symptoms such as anxiety or insomnia.

Special Considerations

Lower doses may be required for elderly patients due to increased sensitivity to benzodiazepines. Extra caution is advised for individuals with liver impairment or a history of substance misuse.

Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

Use during pregnancy is not recommended due to potential risk to the unborn baby. Alprazolam may pass into breast milk, so breastfeeding should be avoided while taking this medication.

Side Effects

Common side effects include sleepiness, dizziness, fatigue, and memory or concentration difficulties. These effects are usually more noticeable at the start of treatment and may lessen over time.

Overdose Warning

Symptoms of overdose may include extreme drowsiness, confusion, poor coordination, and slowed breathing. Immediate emergency medical attention is required if an overdose is suspected.

Storage Instructions

Store below 30°C in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep this medication out of the reach of children.

FAQ about anxiety and panic disorder medicines

What medicines are commonly used for anxiety and panic disorder?

Doctors may prescribe antidepressants, beta blockers, or short-term calming medicines depending on the patient’s symptoms, health history, and treatment needs.

Can anxiety medicine help with panic attacks?

Yes, some prescribed medicines may help reduce the frequency, severity, and sudden impact of panic attacks when used under proper medical guidance.

How long does anxiety medicine take to work?

Some anxiety medicines may take a few weeks to show full results, while fast-acting medicines may provide quicker short-term relief.

Are anxiety and panic disorder medicines safe for daily use?

Some medicines are suitable for daily use, but others should only be used short term because they may cause side effects, tolerance, or dependence.

Can I stop taking anxiety medicine suddenly?

No, stopping suddenly may cause withdrawal symptoms or make anxiety or panic symptoms worse, so patients should always follow medical advice.

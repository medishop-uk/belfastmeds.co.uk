FAQ about online pharmacies

Is an online pharmacy legal in Belfast?

Yes, online pharmacy services can operate legally when they follow UK and Northern Ireland pharmacy rules. Customers should choose services that ask for proper health information and prescription details where required.

Can I buy prescription medicine online in the UK?

Prescription medicines can be requested online, but they should only be supplied after a valid prescription or suitable clinical review. A responsible pharmacy will not supply prescription-only medicines without proper checks.

How does an online prescription process work?

You usually choose a treatment, complete a health form, provide prescription details where needed, and wait for a suitability review. If the request is approved, the medicine can be prepared and dispatched.

Can online pharmacies deliver discreetly?

Many online pharmacies offer discreet delivery to protect customer privacy. This is helpful for sensitive treatment areas such as anxiety, sleep concerns, and prescription-based medicine needs.

What should I check before ordering medicine online?

Check that the service follows pharmacy standards, asks for health details, provides clear medicine information, and does not offer prescription-only medicines without review. Avoid websites that promise instant supply with no checks.
